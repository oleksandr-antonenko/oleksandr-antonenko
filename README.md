# Oleksandr Antonenko

**Product (Software) Engineer.** I make businesses more efficient and more profitable with software, and I talk business, not tech.

Idea → Agentic SDLC → Production · Vilnius, Lithuania · 15+ years in software, 10+ leading teams

I take the processes a business already runs — orders, stock, invoicing, delivery — and turn them into a system that does the work. Then I judge it the way an owner does: did orders stop leaking, did the month get cheaper, did a seven-day job turn into a two-hour one.
